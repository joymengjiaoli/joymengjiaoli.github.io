About Me

I am an LL.M. candidate in European and International Law at Saarland University. I have an academic background in criminal procedure law and practical experience in juvenile prosecution and legal practice in China.

My work focuses on the protection of human rights within European and international legal systems, with particular attention to children’s rights and juvenile justice.

Research Interests

European Human Rights Law · Children’s Rights · Juvenile Justice · European Public Law · Privacy and Data Protection · Business and Human Rights
